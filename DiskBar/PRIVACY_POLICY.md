anka # Privacy Policy for DiskBar

**Effective Date:** March 30, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how **DiskBar** ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through complete transparency.

## 1. Personal Data Collection

**DiskBar does not collect, store, or process any personal data.**
We do not ask for, gather, or record any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, IP address, or location.

## 2. Local Processing of System Data

DiskBar accesses hardware and system metrics, including:

- Disk status and drive information
- S.M.A.R.T. (Self-Monitoring, Analysis, and Reporting Technology) diagnostic data
- Storage temperature readings
- Disk usage and performance stats

**All such data is processed strictly locally on your device in real-time.** It is used solely to render the hardware monitoring user interface on your machine and is never saved to external locations or logs.

## 3. No Server Transmission

DiskBar operates entirely offline with respect to telemetry and system data. **No information, telemetry, usage statistics, or system metrics are ever sent to external servers or cloud services.**

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

Because DiskBar does not collect or transmit any data, **we do not sell, rent, trade, or share any information with third parties**, advertising agencies, or analytics services.

## 5. System Access & Permissions

To function properly, DiskBar accesses necessary system information and hardware APIs on your device (e.g., disk drive metrics and temperature sensors). These permissions are strictly utilized locally to deliver real-time system monitoring features to you.
